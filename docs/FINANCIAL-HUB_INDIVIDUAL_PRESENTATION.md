# PROBLEM STATEMENT

## Financial Gaps in Kenya

Kenya has made remarkable progress in expanding financial services, yet important gaps remain in financial health, consumer protection, productive credit access, and long-term resilience.

- Kenya processes over **KES 35 trillion (~USD 270B)** annually through mobile money — yet savings rates remain **below 12%**
- **68%** of salaried Kenyans run out of money before month-end
- **5.1 million** Kenyans are trapped in digital loan cycles *(FSD Kenya, 2025; Central Bank of Kenya, 2026)*
- **96%** of formal-sector workers earn less than KES 100,000/month, and only **2.65%** of bank accounts hold more than KES 100,000

## The Individual Challenge

Access to finance is only part of the problem. Income arrives as a **single lump-sum balance** — rent, food, transport, school fees and savings all look like one spendable number.

The result is a cognitive framing problem, not financial illiteracy: people perceive the entire balance as immediately available, spend reactively, and remain **one pay slip away from financial distress**.

## The Digital vs. Discipline Paradox

Digital payments are accelerating — moving money faster than ever, and generating more financial data than ever before.

Yet financial *outcomes* are not improving: no tool restructures how money is managed at the moment it arrives. Mobile money optimized how fast money moves — not how well it is managed.

# THE SOLUTION: FINANCIAL HUB

## Every shilling arrives with a job.

Financial Hub is an intelligent financial operating system for individuals. Instead of tracking spending after it happens, it **splits income into purpose-bound pockets the moment it arrives** — before a lump-sum balance can ever be seen.

**Flow diagram:** Incoming Income (M-Pesa / Bank / Cash) → **Financial Hub — Automatic Allocation** → five destination cards:

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Savings** | **Fixed Costs** | **Daily Pockets** | **Loans & Goals** |
| Minimum 10%, time-locked, protected | Rent, bills, fees — itemized & earmarked | Food, transport, leisure — paced by daily caps | Repayment schedules with on-time scoring |

*Unspent daily amounts roll over into Savings automatically at midnight.*

**Three pillars (sidebar):**

- **Intelligent Organization** — Income is automatically split into structured pockets with per-category spending rules, creating mental accounting that makes overspending structurally difficult.
- **Behavioral Design** — Commitment devices, cooling-off friction, streaks and a discipline score shift money management from reactive cash handling to proactive planning — no willpower required.
- **Measurable Outcomes** — Protected savings, visible discipline, and a behavioral track record that builds credit readiness with SACCOs, banks and lenders.

# THE PRODUCT: A PLAN FOR EVERY EARNER

## Users never build budgets. Behavior builds the plan.

A 6-step behavioral check-in (income pattern → spending habits → life stage → savings goal → fixed costs) feeds a **rules engine** that infers the right financial plan — with transparent reasoning the user can see and trust.

**Four plan personas, assigned automatically:**

- **Salaried — Structured** — monthly allocation into pockets; fixed costs earmarked, savings locked, spendable budgeted by category
- **Salaried — Daily Budget** — for tight budgets & spender habits: every spendable pocket gets a daily cap
- **Freelancer / Gig — Daily Budget** — daily caps adapt to the user's real income rhythm (weekly / biweekly / irregular), not a fixed calendar month
- **Student — Simplified** — one simple daily spend pocket; no overwhelming category splits

**The Home screen leads with one number: "Safe to spend today."**
Not the raw balance — the amount that is genuinely free after rent, savings and obligations are protected. Food money can never secretly eat into transport money.

# THE BEHAVIORAL INTELLIGENCE LAYER

## The moat: we don't track money. We make it behave.

Every feature is an applied behavioral-finance mechanism, live in the product today:

- **Commitment devices** — Savings time-locked 30–90 days by goal; early unlock requires biometrics and costs discipline points; goal completion unlocks for free
- **Cooling-off friction** — Moving essential money to leisure triggers a 1-hour pause, tuned to the user's money personality (spender / saver / avoider); skipping costs 5 disclosed points
- **Discipline score (0–100)** — A transparent points economy: under-cap days +3, streak milestones +5, on-time loan repayments +5, full repayment +10, overspend −3, gambling attempts −5, overdraft override −10 — with monthly caps so one bad week never spirals
- **Streaks & rollover** — Unspent daily budgets sweep into Savings at midnight; under-cap streaks with milestones at 3/7/14/30/60/90 days and one forgiveness freeze per month
- **Smart guardrails** — Category-scoped pockets (rent money can only pay rent), gambling blocked from every pocket with no override, daily-cap overrides that show tomorrow's reduced cap *before* confirming
- **Adaptive intelligence** — A monthly planning cycle learns from actual spending and recommends allocation adjustments with confidence levels; nudges project which pockets will run dry before they do

# BUILT FOR EVERY KENYAN EARNER

## One system, three lives

- **The salaried professional** — "I earn KES 60,000 on the 28th. By the 20th I'm borrowing." → Fixed costs earmarked on payday, 10% savings locked automatically, and a single honest number for everything else
- **The gig worker / freelancer** — "Income comes in bursts — good week, dead month." → Daily budgets derived from actual runway (days of spending power), a monthly planning cycle that re-plans from real income, and an emergency unlock priced in runway days, once a month at most
- **The student / first earner** — "Budgeting apps are overwhelming." → One simple daily pocket, gentle guardrails, and a streak that makes discipline feel like progress, not punishment

# REGULATORY COMPLIANCE, RISK & IP

## Regulatory Compliance & Risk Management

- **Financial Infrastructure** — Integrates through secure APIs with licensed mobile money, banks & SACCOs. The current build records and orchestrates; it does not move money
- **Custody & Funds Risk** — Financial Hub does **not hold customer funds**; it securely automates authorized allocations across users' existing regulated financial accounts, with a complete double-entry-style transaction ledger as the source of truth
- **Privacy by Design** — Compliant with Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 & ODPC. Bank-grade security: row-level database isolation, biometric app lock, rate limiting, and audited access
